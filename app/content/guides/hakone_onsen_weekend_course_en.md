Hakone is close enough to Tokyo for a day soak, and rich enough for an overnight that feels like a different country. This course is built for travelers who want a clear sequence — arrival, soak, stay (optional), and a second bath — without inventing a new plan from scratch.

## Who this is for

*   First-timers who want **one coherent Hakone loop**, not a random list of hotels
*   Couples or friends who can share a private bath (*kashikiri*) if tattoos or privacy matter
*   Anyone willing to use the **Hakone Free Pass** and buses instead of a rental car

## Day 1 — Arrive, soak, settle in Gora

**Morning:** Odakyu Romancecar from Shinjuku to Hakone-Yumoto (~85 minutes). Buy or activate the Hakone Free Pass before you leave Tokyo if you plan multiple local rides.

**Late morning / lunch:** Walk the Yumoto core, eat lightly, and confirm your overnight check-in time. If you are day-tripping only, skip ahead to a day-use bath and return the same evening.

**Afternoon soak + stay (pick one lane):**

*   **Modern luxury lane:** [Hotel Indigo Hakone Gora](/onsen/hotel_indigo_hakone_gora_en) — private baths, design-forward, easy base in Gora.
*   **Imperial garden lane:** [Hakone Gora Kadan](/onsen/hakone_gora_kadan_en) — Relais & Châteaux, in-room rotenburo, once-in-a-trip energy.
*   **Lake view lane:** [The Prince Hakone Lake Ashinoko](/onsen/the_prince_hakone_lake_ashinoko_en) — broader resort feel with Ashinoko scenery.

Check tattoo and private-bath policies on each detail page before you book.

## Day 2 — Ropeway views, second soak, return

**Morning:** Tozan Railway or bus toward Gora → ropeway toward Owakudani / Togendai if the weather is clear. Keep expectations flexible; volcanic steam and cloud cover change hourly.

**Second soak options:** Use your ryokan’s morning bath, or add a day-use facility near Yumoto before the Romancecar home. Confirm last-train timing back to Shinjuku.

**If you only have one day:** Romancecar in → one strong day-use or short ryokan bath block → lakeside or Gora stroll → Romancecar out. Quality of one long soak beats three rushed dips.

## Packing & etiquette reminders

*   Small towel + larger towel if the facility expects you to bring your own
*   Waterproof tattoo cover patches if needed
*   Cash for smaller shops; cards are common at hotels but not universal in town
