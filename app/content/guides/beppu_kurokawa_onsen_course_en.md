Kyushu’s onsen story is easier if you treat **Beppu as the open-air spectacle** and **Kurokawa as the intimate riverside stay**. This course assumes you can spare 2–3 nights and will move by train/bus or a short rental-car hop.

## Sketch

1.  Arrive via Oita / Fukuoka → **Beppu** (1 night)
2.  Transfer to **Kurokawa Onsen** (1–2 nights)
3.  Exit toward Kumamoto / Fukuoka

## Beppu — hells by day, baths by night

Beppu is for geothermal sightseeing plus a solid hotel soak. Start with the hells tour if you have never seen boiling mud pools, then return to your hotel’s baths when the day crowds thin.

Suggested OKOnsen anchors:

*   [Beppu Daiiti Hotel](/onsen/beppu_daiiti_hotel_en)
*   [Beppu Showaen](/onsen/beppu_showaen_en)

Read the [Beppu hells tour guide](/guide/beppu_hell_tour_guide_en) for pacing. Keep lunch light — the afternoon steam and evening soak land better that way.

## Kurokawa — slow lanes and outdoor baths

Kurokawa rewards walking between public baths with a *nyuto tegata* (bath passport) if you are day-hopping, or settling into one ryokan for dinner and night baths if you want stillness.

Suggested stays:

*   [Kurokawa Onsen Fujiya](/onsen/kurokawa_onsen_fujiya_en)
*   [Kurokawa Onsen Shinmeikan](/onsen/kurokawa_onsen_shinmeikan_en)
*   [Kurokawa Onsen Nanjoen](/onsen/kurokawa_onsen_nanjoen_en)
*   [Kurokawa Onsen Hozantei](/onsen/kurokawa_onsen_hozantei_en)
*   [Kurokawa Onsen Gosho Gekkoju](/onsen/kurokawa_onsen_gosho_gekkoju_en)

For hidden corners, see [Kurokawa hidden gems](/guide/kurokawa_hidden_gems_en).

## Logistics notes

*   Beppu ↔ Kurokawa is not a subway hop — budget transfer time and confirm last buses
*   Kurokawa evenings are quiet; book dinner-inclusive ryokan stays when possible
*   Tattoo policies vary — prefer Private Bath filters on the map when needed
