## Introduction

If the main Kusatsu streets feel busy, **Konoha** is the “one block quieter” option: still in Kusatsu Onsen, still using the town’s famous water, but oriented toward **private family baths (*kashikiri*)** and scenic outdoor soaking rather than a mega-hotel lobby scene.

## Baths

Expect Kusatsu’s characteristic strong spring water in outdoor and indoor facilities, plus bookable private baths — useful for families, couples, or guests who prefer not to use communal areas. Confirm hours and fees for *kashikiri* when you check in.

## Stay & food

Rooms lean cozy and nature-facing. Meals are typically kaiseki-style dinner with a proper Japanese breakfast. Flag allergies and spice preferences early.

## Who it suits

*   Travelers who want **Private Bath** first, spectacle second
*   Guests who will still walk to the yubatake for sightseeing, then return for a quieter night soak
*   Winter visitors chasing steam-and-snow outdoor baths (dress warmly between facilities)
