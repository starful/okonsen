## Introduction

**Yufuin** trades dense onsen-town alleys for valley views and café streets under **Mount Yufu**. **Baien** sits in that landscape as a **luxury ryokan** stop: large outdoor baths, private *kashikiri* options, and the kind of dinner pacing that makes a one-night Kyushu detour feel complete.

## Baths & views

The draw is soaking with a wide view toward the Yufuin basin and Mt. Yufu when the weather cooperates. Private family baths help if you want uninterrupted time or need more privacy than communal halls allow. Always confirm tattoo rules before arrival.

## Dining & rooms

Expect seasonal kaiseki dinner and a full Japanese breakfast. Rooms emphasize quiet finishes and garden or mountain aspects — ask for a view-facing category if that is your priority.

## Getting there

*   From **Fukuoka**: highway bus or train + local transfer into Yufuin
*   From **Beppu**: a natural second stop on a Kyushu onsen loop (see the [Beppu & Kurokawa course](/guide/beppu_kurokawa_onsen_course_en) for a wider Kyushu frame)
