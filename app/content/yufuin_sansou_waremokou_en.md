## Introduction

**Yufuin Sansou Waremokou (由布院 山荘 蕨野)** is for travelers who want the soak **in the room**, not only in a shared bathhouse. Many categories include a private open-air bath with a Mount Yufu aspect — the signature reason people choose this property over a town-center hotel.

## Baths & privacy

In-room *rotenburo* change the rhythm of a stay: bathe before dinner, at midnight, or at dawn without watching the clock on a *kashikiri* reservation. Shared or larger baths may still exist on property — check the current layout on the detail page and at booking.

Confirm tattoo, pet, and children’s policies directly; they can change by season and room type.

## Food & setting

Kaiseki dinner and Japanese breakfast are the norm. The setting is hillside and quieter than Yunotsubo Avenue’s densest stretch — plan a short transfer or walk if you want café and gallery hopping during the day.

## Trip pairing

*   **One night from Fukuoka** for a soft landing into Kyushu onsen culture
*   **After Beppu** if you want spectacle first, then a private-bath night in Yufuin
